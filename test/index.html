Straight with you

What we won't do.

Neala doesn't diagnose you, and we're upfront about why: your Neala pattern is based on your self-reported answers, not a clinical assessment. Think of it as "based on what you've told us, your pattern points to…" — not a medical verdict.

Common questions

Before you download.

Is Neala a medical app?

No. Neala uses your self-reported answers to identify patterns and build recommendations — it's not a diagnostic or clinical tool. Always check with your doctor for medical concerns, especially around HRT, supplements, or new or worsening symptoms.

How does the pattern assessment work?

A short onboarding quiz asks about your symptoms, history and lifestyle. Your answers are scored against four hormonal patterns, and you're matched to the one that fits best.

What if I don't fit neatly into one pattern?

Most women lean toward one dominant pattern even when a few symptoms overlap. Your plan is built around your dominant pattern, with room to adjust as you log more data.

Will the coach replace my doctor?

No. It's a daily companion for lifestyle coaching — nutrition, movement, symptoms and supplements — not a substitute for clinical care.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your information is encrypted and never sold. You decide what the app tracks and remembers. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Do I need to count calories?

No. Neala uses plate composition and portion guidance instead of calorie counting.

Do I need a gym?

No. Strength-first training, three full-body sessions a week for most patterns — two for Wired but Tired, building up as energy stabilises. Dumbbells, bands and bodyweight are all you need.

What if I'm on HRT or medication?

Neala flags supplement interactions and always recommends checking with your doctor or pharmacist before starting anything new.

Can I use Neala if I don't have obvious symptoms?

Yes — the Futureproof pattern is built for exactly this: low symptom burden now, with a plan focused on prevention.
