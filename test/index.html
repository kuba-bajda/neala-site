Our story

Why we built this.

Menopause is universal. The way it shows up isn't. Yet the advice available is still mostly generic — written for “menopausal women” as a single group, rather than for the different hormonal mechanisms actually driving different women's symptoms. Neala exists to close that gap: personalised, profile-first coaching for a life stage that deserves more than one-size-fits-all.

Common questions

Before you download.

How does the profile assessment work?

A short onboarding quiz asks about your symptoms, history and lifestyle. Your answers are scored against multiple menopause profiles, and you're matched to the one that fits best.

What if I don't fit neatly into one profile?

Most women lean toward one dominant profile even when a few symptoms overlap. Your plan is built around your dominant profile, with room to adjust as you log more data.

Will the coach replace my doctor?

No. It's a daily companion for lifestyle coaching — nutrition, movement and symptoms — not a substitute for clinical care.

Do I need a gym?

No. Strength-first training, three full-body sessions a week for most profiles — fewer at the start if your energy is low, building up as it stabilises. Dumbbells, bands and bodyweight are all you need.

Can I use Neala if I don't have obvious symptoms?

Yes — one of our profiles is built for exactly this: low symptom burden now, with a plan focused on prevention.
