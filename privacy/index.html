Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

Neala is operated by Meridian Studios sp. z o.o., Poland ("we", "us"), the controller of your personal data. Contact: hello@neala.co.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, sign-in details and settings
  • Health and wellbeing data (special category data under GDPR Article 9): information you enter about your health, lifestyle and goals, your progress, and your coach conversations
  • Subscription data: your plan and status. Payments are handled by Apple or Google.
  • Device and usage data: device and app information, app usage and error reports
  • Information you send us: feedback, support requests, optional answers, mailing-list sign-ups and messages on social media

3. How we use it and why

  • To provide and personalise the app, including coaching and reminders: your explicit consent, given in the app before any health data is collected (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), and our contract with you
  • To manage your subscription: our contract with you
  • To run analytics and fix errors: your consent
  • To respond to you, measure our marketing and improve Neala: our legitimate interests. We never use health data for advertising.

You don't have to give us health data, but without it we can't provide the app. Your plan is personalised automatically from your answers; this has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run Neala (hosting, AI, analytics, error monitoring, payments, notifications, team communication and social media), under contracts that require them to protect it. We do not sell your data.

If you allow it, Neala reads step data from Apple Health. Apple Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties except to provide the app.

Our website

neala.co uses Google Analytics cookies only if you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice with Cookie settings at the bottom of any page.

5. International transfers

Your data is stored in the EU. Some providers are in the United States; these transfers are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. Analytics and error data are kept for up to 12 months and website analytics for up to 14 months. Other data is kept until you ask us to delete it or it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, restrict, export or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. To delete your account and data, use "Delete account" in the app's Settings, or email hello@neala.co. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in the App Store or Google Play. For any other request, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is restricted.

9. Children

Neala is for adults aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

We will tell you in the app about material changes. The date above shows the current version.
